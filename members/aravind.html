Aravind Parthasarathy

Principal Investigator, Assistant Professor, Department of Communication Science and Disorders, University of Pittsburgh

Biography

Dr. Aravind Parthasarathy is the principal investigator of the Parthasarathy Lab as well as an assistant professor at the University of Pittsburgh. He is interested in understanding how the peripheral auditory system and the central auditory pathway interact in various forms of hearing loss. His overall goals through his research is to inform diagnosis and track the benefits of interventional therapies in clinicial populations with hearing loss by utilizing insights obtained from animal models with similar forms of pathology.
