Luz Stephanie Andrino

Graduate Student - PSN, Carnegie-Mellon Univversity

Biography

Luz is a Systems Neuroscience PhD candidate at Carnegie Mellon University. She received her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from California State University, Northridge, and then completed an NIH-funded postbaccalaureate program at Purdue University studying somatosensory systems and brain-machine interfaces. Her research interests are in the peripheral effects of hidden hearing loss. Outside of the lab, she enjoys volunteering with Latinas in PGH, reading, and live music (from the symphony to punk rock concerts).
