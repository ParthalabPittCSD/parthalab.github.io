Aravind Receives R01 Grant

Dr. Aravindakshan Parthasarathy received his R01 grant from the National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders. His research at the University of Pittsburgh investigates the neural encording of temporal fine structure (TFS) influences speech perception and listening effort in noisy, multi-talker environments. TFS cues are rapid sound fluctuations encoded through precise neural timing in the auditory system. Findings will define the role of TFS coding and support future diagnostics and interventions for individuals with speech-in-noise difficulties despite normal audiograms and inform cochlear implace and hearing aid advancements. Read more about his research in upcoming papers!
